**Brag** shows a big banner and takes a screenshot whenever you:

- level up
- turn in a quest
- loot an epic or legendary item

**Posting to Discord needs a small companion script.** WoW addons can't access the internet, so the addon only takes the screenshot. The companion script runs on your PC, watches your Screenshots folder and uploads each new screenshot to a Discord webhook within a few seconds.

Get the companion and setup steps here: <LINK TO GITHUB / DOWNLOAD PAGE>

Notes:
- Screenshots you take yourself (Print Screen) are posted too while the companion runs.
- Without the companion, Brag still takes the screenshots; nothing gets posted.
